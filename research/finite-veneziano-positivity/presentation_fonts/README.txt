Presentation font only. This is a subset of installed Noto Sans CJK SC Regular,
converted from cubic to quadratic outlines for ReportLab TrueType embedding.
Font family name: Noto Sans SC Summary. Outlines and widths retain the source design.
Source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
